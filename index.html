RentalPrep · Schedule E Workpaper Builder

Turns a client's rental property records into a reviewed Schedule E, depreciation schedule, and passive loss summary.

1Client & filing information

Used for the passive activity loss limit (Form 8582) and the workpaper header.

2Rental properties

One tab per property, the same way Schedule E uses columns A, B, C.

3Workpaper

Updates as you type. Review the flags before the numbers go into tax software.

RentalPrep is a student-built preparation aid for a tax associate's workflow. It does not replace professional judgment or tax software, and every output should be reviewed before filing. Depreciation uses regular MACRS (GDS); bonus depreciation, Section 179, dispositions, and the mid-quarter convention are flagged for review instead of calculated.
